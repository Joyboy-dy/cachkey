# Visual polish checklist

## Hierarchy

- Primary title/action are obvious without overpowering content.
- Supporting text is quieter but readable.
- Sections are grouped by spacing before borders/cards are added.

## Rhythm

- Repeated gaps and padding follow existing tokens or a small consistent scale.
- Baselines and edges align across adjacent components.

## Typography

- Heading levels are visually distinct and semantically sensible.
- Body line lengths remain readable.
- Numeric/tabular content aligns appropriately.

## States

- Loading does not cause avoidable layout jumps.
- Empty/error states explain the next useful action.
- Hover/focus/selected/disabled states remain coherent.

## Responsive

- Priority survives reflow.
- Tables/controls have a deliberate small-screen strategy.
- No essential action becomes unreachable.
